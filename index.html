Algorithm Deep Dive

Understand how each algorithm approaches the clustering problem

K-Means Graph

Projects nodes into a feature space (using degree, betweenness centrality, and clustering coefficient) then applies iterative centroid-based partitioning. Fast convergence with O(nk) complexity.

O(nk·iter)Partitionalk required

Spectral Clustering

Constructs the normalized graph Laplacian, computes the k smallest eigenvectors, and embeds nodes into a low-dimensional eigenspace before running k-means. Excellent for non-convex clusters.

O(n³)Spectralk required

Louvain Method

Greedily optimizes modularity Q in two phases: local node reassignment and community contraction. Produces hierarchical communities naturally and scales to millions of nodes.

O(n log n)ModularityHierarchical

DBSCAN

Identifies core nodes with ≥minPts neighbors within ε-radius and expands clusters via reachability. Naturally detects noise/outlier nodes and requires no prior knowledge of cluster count.

O(n log n)DensityNoise-aware

Girvan-Newman

Iteratively removes edges with highest betweenness centrality, progressively splitting the graph into communities. Produces a full dendrogram and excels at detecting bridge-heavy communities.

O(m²n)DivisiveDendrogram
